Featured Entry
On the Doctrine of Signatures
The old herbalists believed that plants announce their purpose by their appearance — walnut resembles the brain, lungwort the lung, eyebright the eye. Whether literally true or not, this is a powerful framework for noticing. What does a thing look like it wants to do? This is pattern recognition dressed in poetry, and I think both readings are valid at once.
