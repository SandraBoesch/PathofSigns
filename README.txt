Path of Signs Mini App v2

How to use:
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
3. Crystal cards are compact.
4. Click each accordion section to open details and add notes.
5. Use the image upload on each crystal card to add your own stone photo.
6. Click Save or click out of a field. Notes are saved in your browser.

Important:
- Your data is saved locally in that browser using localStorage.
- Before changing computers, open Backup / Restore and click Download Backup.
- On the new computer, open the app, go to Backup / Restore, and restore the JSON backup file.
- Images are saved inside your browser data. Smaller/cropped photos are best.
